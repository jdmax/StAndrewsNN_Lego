    How it goes together

    • Walls are two bricks thick: tan brick outside, white plaster inside, with a clear clerestory band on top.
    • The steep roof is a stair of dark grey 45° slopes backed by medium nougat inverted slopes, so the underside reads as the wood ceiling.
    • The roof splits into seven modules at the ribs. The five middle ones are identical and lift straight off the knee walls to show the interior.
    • Ribs are reddish brown slope stairs standing on the arcade posts, one stud thick, with a small gap below the ceiling so the roof lifts freely.
    • The aisles have a flat medium nougat ceiling at the top of the glass band; the low aisle roofs are fixed.

    Still to decide or check

    • Width, length and roof-break height come from the photo massing model and are the measurements most worth taking.
    • Rear gable outside is plain white because the photos didn't show it.
    • The one-stud ribs may flex sideways; a hidden Technic brace or a second stud of thickness would stiffen them.
    • Some colour and part combinations (medium nougat inverted slopes, sand blue bricks in bulk, trans-coloured 1 × 2s) need checking for availability.
    • Not modelled yet: pendant lamps, the small crosses over the narthex doorway, the brocade falls, organ pipes behind the grilles.
